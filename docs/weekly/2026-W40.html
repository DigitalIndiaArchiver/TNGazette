What this means for you

  • Madras High Court issued new 2026 rules for Presidency Small Cause Courts covering how penalties are adjudicated and appeals handled.
  • Tamil Nadu bans manufacture, storage, distribution and sale of gutkha, pan masala and chewable tobacco products for one year from 23 May 2026.
  • Council of States bye-election announced after C. Ve. Shanmugam's resignation: poll dates fixed, Returning Officer and Assistant Returning Officer appointed.
  • Land acquisition notices (Form C) issued under the Tamil Nadu Acquisition of Land for Industrial Purposes Act, 1997; affected owners should watch for notices.
  • Legislative Assembly seats of four members declared vacant following resignations; appointment of Speaker and Deputy Speaker also notified.
  • New ministers appointed for Tamil Nadu and subjects allocated among ministers through Public Department orders.
  • Home Department amended the Tamil Nadu Cinemas (Regulation) Rules, 1957.
  • Ordinary gazette this week: only personal name changes, private advertisements and company-law notifications - nothing affecting daily life.
