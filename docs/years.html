Year by year

Every year of the Tamil Nadu Government Gazette in this archive, 2008–2026. Counts are documents (unique PDFs), not listing rows — where the official site lists one PDF under several headings, it is counted once.

Numbers are links: each count opens the exact dataset file or text folder it came from. W/E under each year open the government's own listing pages for that year (weekly / extraordinary).

Reading the table

  • Weekly parts / issues — the Ordinary Gazette, published once a week. Parts are the individual PDF documents inside each issue (name changes, notifications, advertisements); issues are the weekly editions themselves.
  • Extraordinary — urgent one-off publications (ordinances, new statutes, appointments, land acquisitions), numbered continuously within a year.
  • Text layer — PDFs whose full text has been extracted to Markdown, shown as ordinary + extraordinary. Every text file links back to its source PDF, so treat the text as a search index over the original.

Why some cells are empty

  • 2024–2025 weekly index is missing. The government redesigned its site around 2024 and the new listing only exposes a recent window of issues. Recovering those years needs the Wayback Machine layer — tracked as an open task.
  • 2025 extraordinary is text-only. The notification index for 2025 was scraped and its text extracted, but the CSV alone is ~186 MB — over GitHub's 100 MB file cap — so the index is not published here yet. The text layer (with its links back to the source PDFs) is fully available.
  • 2026 is in progress. The weekly window has been backfilled to January 2026; extraordinary coverage currently starts in April. Both grow with the weekly pipeline.
  • 2023 is a hinge year. The old site format was scraped until October 2023; the redesign then interrupts the series until the 2026 backfill.
  • 2008 starts mid-year. The earliest data in the archive begins with the government's own e-gazette rollout, not the start of the calendar year.

Everything listed here is fetched from the official Stationery and Printing Department site and preserved on the Internet Archive — see Originals & archives for how that layer works.
