What this means for you

  • Registration fees amended: the Commercial Taxes and Registration Department changed the fee table under the Registration Act, 1908 — affects document registration charges.
  • Correction issued to amendments of the Tamil Nadu Legal Metrology (Enforcement) Rules, 2011 — Labour department; these rules cover weights and measures.
  • Temple employees: service-condition rules for Hindu Religious Institutions staff (2020 Rules) were amended — affects working conditions of temple employees.
  • Elections: a Madras High Court judgment in Election Petition No.36 of 2026 was published in the Gazette.
  • RTI: the HRM Department issued a notification under the Right to Information Act, 2005.
  • Name changes: one ordinary gazette lists individuals' name changes (Tamil and English) plus private advertisements and company-law notices — names only, no policy change.
